\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Reward supervision for reasoning.}
RLHF learns preference rewards~\citep{ouyang2022training}, whereas RLVR uses programmatic answer checks~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath,deepseekr1,lambert2024tulu3,zeng2025simplerlzoo}; subsequent objectives improve optimization and credit propagation~\citep{yu2025dapo,kazemnejad2025vineppo}. Terminal rewards, however, cannot distinguish differently supported traces with the same answer and remain vulnerable to gaming~\citep{gao2023scaling,skalse2022defining,gao2024designing}. PRMs provide denser feedback through human labels~\citep{uesato2022solving,lightman2023lets}, rollout or search~\citep{wang2024math,luo2024omegaprm,zhang2024rest}, induced annotations~\citep{lu2024autopsv}, outcome-derived values~\citep{yuan2024implicitprm,cui2026prime}, or generative verification~\citep{thinkprm2025,genprm2026}; PRMBench and ProcessBench nevertheless reveal persistent error-detection and generalization failures~\citep{song2025prmbench,zheng2024processbench}. Scalar completion, step, or prefix scores summarize reasoning quality without explicitly identifying the non-adjacent premises supporting a conclusion. \textit{\method complements answer verification with auditable local-continuity and non-local-support signals extracted from free text.}

\paragraph{Structured reasoning representations.}
Tree of Thoughts and Graph of Thoughts expose branching search and dependencies~\citep{yao2023tree,besta2024graph}; PARC represents explicit premise links~\citep{mukherjee2025parc}, while DAG-Math and GraphPRM operate on graph-formatted derivations or trajectories~\citep{zhang2026dagmath,peng2025graphprm}. Correctness-stratified clipping has also been studied for graph-formatted reasoning~\citep{liu2026graph}. \textit{\method instead extracts evidence-bearing support from ordinary reasoning text and uses it for training-time credit assignment and targeted revision, without changing the policy output or inference interface.}

\paragraph{Reasoning distillation and compression.}
Knowledge distillation transfers teacher behavior~\citep{hinton2015distilling}; language-model variants address exposure bias through reverse-KL, on-policy, adaptive off-policy, Best-of-$N$, black-box, or reward-coupled objectives~\citep{minillm2023,agarwal2024gkd,ko2024distillm,sessa2024bond,xu2025kdrl,ye2025gad,yang2026exopd}. Reasoning compression controls budgets, prunes tokens, tunes for length, or shapes efficiency rewards~\citep{snell2024com,muennighoff2025s1,xia2025tokenskip,luo2025o1,arora2026training,aggarwal2025l1}; structured variants prune reasoning DAGs or distill semantic operations~\citep{yuan2026graphpruning,wen2026scaffolding}. \textit{\method uses defects detected in the student's own trace to target teacher revision, while answer, structure, and response budget jointly determine which revisions become training targets.}
