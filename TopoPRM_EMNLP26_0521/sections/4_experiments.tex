\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
We conduct extensive experiments to evaluate our methods on mathematical reasoning and general tasks including 9 benchmarks: \texttt{GSM8K}, \texttt{MATH-500}, \texttt{Olympiad}, \texttt{Omni-MATH}, \texttt{AIME 2024}, \texttt{AIME 2025}, \texttt{CNMO 2024}, \texttt{MMLU}, \texttt{GPQA-D}. Ane we compare against more than 10 baselines including reference open-source models and proprietary language models.
Among them, we adopt Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen2.5-7B~\citep{yang2024qwen2_5}, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B~\citep{deepseekr1} as the primary base model. 
Training variants use: (i) LoRA SFT~\citep{vonwerra2022trl,hu2022lora} on $\sim$19.5K DAG-annotated math traces, with 3 epochs, 64 rank, learning rate $5{\times}10^{-6}$ and $\alpha{=}128$.
GRPO is run for 200 steps, 4 generations/prompt with learning rate $5{\times}10^{-6}$, $\beta{=}0.04$, equipped with outcome-only reward or the hierarchical TopoPRM reward (Eq.~\ref{eq:r_total}).
The hybrid topology extractor combines rule templates with batched LLM dependency inference and is invoked asynchronously alongside rollout generation (cost analysis in §\ref{sec:rq3}).
The primary metric is accuracy, while pass@k / maj@5 / prm@5 / token-efficiency are recorded for completeness. Full setting and implementation details are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:details}.
