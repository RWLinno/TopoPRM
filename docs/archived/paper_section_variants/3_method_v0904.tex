\method (Figure~\ref{fig:overview}) is a topology-aware post-training framework whose three stages share one graph encoder $\mathcal{E}_{\phi}$ and one acyclic projection $\Pi$: Stage~I produces parsable traces, Stage~II applies GRPO to a topology-conditioned multi-source reward, and Stage~III runs \framework, where the Stage-II teacher revises student rollouts from defects localized on the projected graph.

\subsection{Topology-Aware Reasoning Representation}
\label{sec:data_processing}

For a problem $x=(q,c)$, let $\pi_\theta$ generate $y=(s_1,\ldots,s_T)$. Outcome-only RLVR observes $r_{\mathrm{out}}(x,y)$ but not support among the steps. \method adds a frozen interface $\mathcal{E}(y)=(\mathcal{G}_y,\mathcal{L}_y)$, where $\mathcal{G}_y$ is a typed support DAG and $\mathcal{L}_y$ records the evidence for each edge. It is training-only: the policy neither emits graph syntax nor invokes $\mathcal{E}$ at inference.
% 对于问题 $x=(q,c)$，令 $\pi_\theta$ 生成 $y=(s_1,\ldots,s_T)$。仅基于结果的 RLVR 仅观测 $r_{\mathrm{out}}(x,y)$，无法获取各步骤间的依赖关系。本方法新增了一个冻结接口 $\mathcal{E}(y)=(\mathcal{G}_y,\mathcal{L}_y)$，其中 $\mathcal{G}_y$ 是带类型的依赖 DAG，$\mathcal{L}_y$ 则记录每条边对应的依据。该接口仅在训练阶段生效：推理阶段策略既不会输出图语法，也不会调用 $\mathcal{E}$。

\paragraph{Evidence-grounded DAG construction.}
The scorer segments reasoning at step, sentence, equation, and discourse boundaries, then records normalized expressions, claim keys, variables, and coarse step types. Within each subproblem, it adds $i\!\to\!j$ for $i<j$ when expression reuse, claim reference, or guarded variable overlap provides evidence; an unsupported derivation may receive a weak edge from $s_{j-1}$. Ledger entries retain endpoints, type, triggering spans, and weight, making rule-level audit intrinsic to the score (Appendix~\ref{app:dag}).
% 评分器会在步骤、句子、公式及语篇边界处对推理内容进行切分，随后记录归一化表达式、主张键、变量以及粗略步骤类型。在每个子问题内部，当存在表达式复用、主张引用或受保护变量重叠作为依据时，若满足i<j的条件，评分器会添加$i\!\to\!j$；对于无依据的推导，可能会从$s_{j-1}$处获得一条弱边。日志条目会保留边的端点、类型、触发跨度以及权重，从而让规则层面的审计成为评分过程的固有组成部分（详见附录~\ref{app:dag}）。

Because every edge points forward, $\mathcal{G}_y$ is acyclic by construction. Direction and acyclicity therefore validate the extraction contract, not mathematical correctness. Rollout-level information comes from graph existence, supported conclusions, agreement with independent judgments, and support completeness beyond local continuity. We do not equate graph validity with deductive validity.
% 由于所有边均指向前方，$\mathcal{G}_y$ 在构造上即为无环图。因此，是方向属性与无环性验证了提取契约，而非数学正确性。策略级信息来源于图的存在性、得到支撑的结论、与独立判断的一致性，以及超出局部连续性的支撑完备性。我们并不将图的有效性等同于演绎有效性。

\paragraph{Complementary structural diagnostics.}
Let $\rho_{\mathrm{orph}}(\mathcal{G}_y)$ be the support-weighted fraction of conclusion nodes lacking sufficient incoming evidence, $\delta(\mathcal{G}_y)$ the fraction of order-consistent edges, and $\kappa(\mathcal{G}_y,\mathcal{G}^{\star})$ the typed-edge F1 against an optional reference graph. We define
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:qtopo}
q_{\mathrm{topo}}(y)=
\frac{\lambda_b\mathbb{1}[|\mathcal{V}|>0]+\lambda_a\mathbb{1}[\mathcal{G}_y\ \mathrm{acyclic}]+\lambda_o\mathbb{1}[\rho_{\mathrm{orph}}=0]+\lambda_d\delta+\lambda_k\kappa}
{\lambda_b+\lambda_a+\lambda_o+\lambda_d+\lambda_k},
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_k=0$ without an aligned reference. Policy optimization uses only rollout graphs because generated and reference steps are not aligned. Under forward-only extraction, direction and acyclicity are guards; graph existence and orphan conclusions drive the varying terms.

Continuity measures local traceability. Let $h_i=1$ when $s_i$ is grounded in the problem or a supported prefix and $\eta(y)=T^{-1}\sum_i h_i$; the scorer maps $\eta$ to $q_{\mathrm{cont}}\in[0,1]$. Thus $q_{\mathrm{topo}}$ captures typed support completeness and $q_{\mathrm{cont}}$ captures stepwise progression, neither as a semantic verifier.

\paragraph{Why the two views are complementary.}
A fluent trace may end in an orphan conclusion, while recovered non-local premises can coexist with an unsupported transition; neither diagnostic determines the other. Both remain inspectable because a score change resolves to an orphan node, typed edge, or grounding decision. This evidence-preserving interface distinguishes \method from graph-formatted decoding and opaque process scores.

\subsection{Correctness-Constrained Topology Optimization}
\label{sec:post_training}

Structural supervision must both rank rollouts and constrain how that order enters the gradient. An additive bonus can compensate for an incorrect answer, whereas a hard gate discards useful variation among failures. We therefore separate scalar preference from gradient admissibility.

\paragraph{Outcome-anchored hierarchical reward.}
The outcome-anchored base combines correctness, format, and a bounded length term,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rbase}
r_{\mathrm{base}}=w_o r_{\mathrm{out}}+w_f r_{\mathrm{fmt}}+w_l r_{\mathrm{len}}.
\end{equation}
Within each GRPO group, $q_{\mathrm{topo}}$ and $q_{\mathrm{cont}}$ are scaled to $\widetilde q_{\mathrm{topo}}$ and $\widetilde q_{\mathrm{cont}}$; a constant component maps to $1/2$. The hierarchical reward is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:r_total}
r_{\mathrm{total}}=\operatorname{clip}_{[0,1]}\!\left(
\max(r_{\mathrm{base}},\epsilon)
\left[1+\alpha\widetilde q_{\mathrm{topo}}+(1-\alpha)\widetilde q_{\mathrm{cont}}\right]\right).
\end{equation}
The multiplicative form preserves an explicit outcome source. In the unclipped regime,
$\partial r_{\mathrm{total}}/\partial\widetilde q_{\mathrm{topo}}=\alpha\max(r_{\mathrm{base}},\epsilon)$ and
$\partial r_{\mathrm{total}}/\partial\widetilde q_{\mathrm{cont}}=(1-\alpha)\max(r_{\mathrm{base}},\epsilon)$, so structural resolution scales with task reward rather than acting as a free-standing bonus. ACE controls the remaining sign risk.

\paragraph{Asymmetric credit assignment.}
ACE adapts correctness-stratified clipping~\citep{liu2026graph} to standardized topology--continuity returns. For correct and incorrect strata $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{W}$, let $z_i^{\mathcal C},z_i^{\mathcal W}$ be within-stratum standardized returns and $\Delta r_{\mathrm{aux}}^{(i)}$ the centered auxiliary contribution. ACE computes
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ace}
\widehat A_i=
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{clip}\!\left(z_i^{\mathcal C}+\max(0,\Delta r_{\mathrm{aux}}^{(i)}),0,\bar c\right), & i\in\mathcal C,\\
\operatorname{clip}\!\left(z_i^{\mathcal W}+\min(0,\Delta r_{\mathrm{aux}}^{(i)}),\underline c,0\right), & i\in\mathcal W.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Stage~I provides a parsable supervised warm start; Stage~II optimizes Eq.~\ref{eq:r_total} with GRPO~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath} using Eq.~\ref{eq:ace}.

\begin{proposition}[Outcome separation with one-sided structural resolution]
\label{prop:ace_separation}
Assume $\underline c\leq0\leq\bar c$. Then $\widehat A_i\in[0,\bar c]$ for $i\in\mathcal C$ and $\widehat A_i\in[\underline c,0]$ for $i\in\mathcal W$. Before saturation, it is monotone only in positive auxiliary deviations on $\mathcal C$ and negative deviations on $\mathcal W$. At fixed standardized return, ACE preserves admissible within-stratum order without reversing correctness-induced gradient signs.
\end{proposition}

Equation~\ref{eq:r_total} defines scalar candidate order; Proposition~\ref{prop:ace_separation} restricts its gradient realization. Appendix~\ref{app:ace_proof} gives the derivation. The \emph{w/o ACE} control retains both reward and TGD, isolating the estimator.

\subsection{Defect-Conditioned Topology-Guided Distillation}
\label{sec:distillation}

Stage~III uses a fixed same-parameter teacher $\pi_T$ and Stage-II target $\pi_\theta$. The target samples $y^{\star}\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid x)$; the scorer converts its highest-priority orphan, missing support, or continuity break into revision instruction $P_r$. The teacher returns $\widetilde y$ and its token distribution conditioned on $(x,y^{\star},P_r)$. Gate $a_r(x,y^{\star},\widetilde y)$ accepts revisions that pass answer, format, structural, and response-budget checks.

\paragraph{Gated distillation objective.}
For target and teacher next-token distributions $p_t$ and $q_t$ on an on-policy prefix, the target minimizes
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:tgd}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{TGD}}(\theta)=
\mathbb{E}_{x,\,y^{\star}\sim\pi_\theta,\,\widetilde y\sim\pi_T}
a_r(x,y^{\star},\widetilde y)\sum_{t=1}^{|\widetilde y|}
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{RKL}}\!\left(p_t\,\|\,q_t\right).
\end{equation}
Unlike static imitation, both dispatch and acceptance depend on current-policy failures, forming a policy--diagnosis--revision--acceptance loop under one structural criterion. Same-scale teacher and target separate behavioral distillation from model compression: all Full checkpoints are post-\tgd, while only marked ablations retain \tgd. Deployment requires neither $\mathcal{E}$, $\pi_T$, nor a separate verifier.

SFT stabilizes the representation, GRPO changes candidate preference, and TGD guides updated-policy failures; one scorer fixes the criterion throughout. Matching $T$ parsed steps costs $O(T^2)$, requires no language-model forward pass, and remains training-only.
