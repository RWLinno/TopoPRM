\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards.}
\textit{\method injects frozen topology and continuity diagnostics from recovered reasoning graphs, so it targets structural credit assignment directly instead of routing it through a correctness surrogate.} Where alignment from human feedback learns a preference model~\citep{ouyang2022training}, RLVR substitutes programmatic correctness checks on objectively verifiable answers~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath,deepseekr1,lambert2024tulu3,zeng2503simplerlzoo}. GRPO-style refinements adjust sampling and clipping, training length, credit granularity, negative samples, entropy, and baselines~\citep{yu2025dapo,liu2025prorl,kazemnejad2025vineppo,zhu2025negsample,cui2025entropy,ahmadian2024back,hu2025reinforce++}, yet all keep an outcome-only signal that stays structurally blind: traces with very different dependency support are reward-equivalent~\citep{chen2026does}, and reward hacking survives a chain that looks benign~\citep{gao2023scaling,skalse2022defining,wang2026trace}. Sharpening the optimizer therefore cannot recover a signal that the reward never carried.
% RLVR 用可程序化验证的答案检查替代了学习型偏好模型，随后一系列 GRPO 改进调整了采样、训练时长、信用粒度、负样本、熵正则与基线。但这些方法的奖励仍只看结果，因而对结构视而不见：依赖支持关系完全不同的轨迹会获得等价奖励，reward hacking 也能在"看起来正常"的推理链下存活。\method 从恢复出的推理图中引入冻结的拓扑与连续性诊断，直接针对结构性信用分配，而不是借正确性代理间接实现。

Length- and efficiency-oriented rewards regulate a different quantity. Decoupled length targets, difficulty-calibrated targets and reflection penalties, prefix-localized token rewards, relative-perplexity preferences, and classifier-monitored step types all control \emph{how much} a model writes rather than \emph{which} step supports which~\citep{li2025drpo,jiang2026decs,liu2026laser,yu2026arlcp,han2026sar,belkhiter2025steptagging}; we reproduce the released DeCS 7B checkpoint under our fixed evaluator. Direction and acyclicity instead separate support relations that can coincide at equal length and unchanged wording, so the two families are orthogonal rather than competing. Multi-source composition remains weighted-sum or correctness-first: mirror-descent reward weighting adapts source weights without representing complementarity~\citep{li2024fairreward}, and HERO stratifies dense scores inside binary verifier outcomes~\citep{tao2026hero}. We keep raw direction and acyclicity as identifiable, interacting sources, train a Shapley-matched additive control, compare additive, multiplicative, and Choquet rankings on one frozen candidate pool, and treat accuracy and length as co-primary outcomes.
% 面向长度与效率的奖励管的是另一件事：解耦长度目标、难度校准目标与反思惩罚、前缀定位的 token 奖励、相对困惑度偏好、分类器监控的步骤类型，控制的是"写多少"而不是"哪一步支持哪一步"；我们在固定评测器下复现了 DeCS 的 7B 开源检查点。方向与无环性区分的是长度相同、措辞不变时仍会不同的支持关系。多源奖励组合目前仍是加权和或正确性优先：镜像下降式权重更新不刻画源之间的互补性，HERO 则在二元验证结果内部分层。我们把方向与无环性保留为可辨识、相互作用的奖励源，训练 Shapley 对齐的加性对照，在同一冻结候选池上比较加性、乘性与 Choquet 排序，并把准确率与长度视为并列主指标。

\paragraph{Process Reward Models for Reasoning.}
\textit{\method trains no scalar neural process verifier and requires no tagged policy format; it derives frozen global-structure supervision from a recovered dependency graph together with local continuity.} PRMs densify sparse outcome rewards, first through dense human step annotation~\citep{uesato2022solving,lightman2023lets}, then through Monte Carlo or tree-search labels~\citep{wang2024math,luo2024omegaprm,zhang2024rest,lu2024autopsv,zhang2025qwen25mathprm,guan2025com}, explicit verifier reasoning~\citep{thinkprm2025,genprm2026,she2025rprm}, intrinsic semantic cues or tagged meta-reasoning actions~\citep{he2026smarter,zhang2026rlvmr}, rollout-induced returns~\citep{setlur2025rewarding,lai2024step}, outcome-only derivation~\citep{yuan2024implicitprm,snell2024com}, and full-prefix conditioning tied to the final outcome~\citep{zhang2026crm}. Benchmarks still expose fine-grained error categories and inter-step inconsistency~\citep{song2025prmbench,zheng2025processbench}. Such scorers can model a prefix, but none recovers an explicit many-to-many support relation among steps: a step is scored given everything written before it, never given the specific non-adjacent premises it actually uses. Deploying them online also adds a trained verifier whose calibration drifts with the policy.
% PRM 用中间步骤打分缓解结果奖励的稀疏性：最初靠密集人工步骤标注，随后用蒙特卡洛或树搜索降低标注成本、用生成式验证器显式写出验证推理、用内在语义线索或显式标注的元推理动作、用 rollout 回报定义步骤价值、只从结果反推隐式步骤分、或以完整前缀为条件并与最终结果关联。基准测试仍暴露出细粒度错误类别与步骤间一致性上的持续弱点。这些打分器能建模前缀，却都没有恢复步骤之间显式的多对多支持关系。\method 不训练标量神经过程验证器、不要求策略输出带标签格式，而是从恢复出的依赖图加局部连续性中获得冻结的全局结构监督。

\paragraph{Structured and Graph-Based Reasoning.}
\textit{\method leaves policy traces in free text and recovers an unconstrained semantic-support graph before projection, so backward and cycle-participating support stay separately measurable and enter the online reward.} Existing structured objectives either constrain the output format or discard exactly those residuals: premise linking and declared-parent verification~\citep{mukherjee2025parc,wen2025grid,fang2026gov}, graph-formatted generation as serialized graph nodes, executable computation graphs, heterogeneous evidence DAGs, or labeled symbolic graphs~\citep{zhang2026dagmath,dong2026structured,anonymous2026structuralcot,jin2026verigraph,liu2026graph,li2026gotr1}, learned verifiers over already-parsed DAGs~\citep{karmore2026atomgraph}, acyclicity as a hard composition gate~\citep{li2026contract}, sanitized claim-support graphs with linearly combined path, cycle, dangling-node, and contradiction terms~\citep{abdullah2026turdpo}, environment-state transition graphs~\citep{feng2026rewardflow}, attention-induced answer-flow DAGs~\citep{dong2026flowtracer}, and credit over predefined relevance or multi-agent communication graphs~\citep{che2026graphgrpo,cang2026graphgrpo}. Declared-parent, attention-DAG, learned-DAG, valid-DAG, and hard-gated representations rule out or never score backward and cyclic semantic support; we retain both as direction and circular-justification signals, and we score them online instead of gating candidates at inference time. Appendix~\ref{app:closest_methods} records the per-method mechanisms and the resulting design boundary.
% 已有的结构化目标要么约束输出格式，要么正好丢掉我们要打分的残差：显式声明父节点并逐节点验证、要求生成图格式（序列化图节点、可执行计算图、异质证据 DAG、带标签的符号图）、在已解析好的 DAG 上学习验证器、把无环性当作硬性组合门、对清洗后的主张—支持图做线性加权、构造环境状态转移图、用注意力导出答案流 DAG、或在预定义关联图与多智能体通信拓扑上传播信用。这些表示都排除或从不打分"逆向与成环的语义支持"。\method 让策略轨迹保持自由文本，在投影前恢复无约束的语义支持图，使逆向与成环支持仍可分别测量并进入在线奖励；逐方法机制与设计边界见附录~\ref{app:closest_methods}。

Post-hoc graph probes confirm non-sequential reasoning~\citep{zhong2026chainsdag,matsutani2026rl} and enable failure analysis, outcome prediction, Best-of-$N$ selection, and structural ensembling~\citep{anonymous2026cot2graph,parulekar2026reasoningconsensus}, but they act after generation, not inside the reward. SARL clusters steps into latent functions and rewards clustering and short paths in their \emph{undirected} transition graph, deliberately abstracting away directional control flow~\citep{wang2026sarl}; that small-world prior is complementary to our semantic-support relation. Hidden-state cycles can reflect useful recurrent visits~\citep{minegishi2025topology}, and self-reinforcing textual repetition is a decoding pathology~\citep{duan2026circular}. Our cycles mean something narrower: circular semantic support in the committed derivation. We penalize neither exploration, nor revision, nor repeated wording, only a final support relation in which a claim depends on itself. The novelty is therefore not ``reasoning as a graph'' but raw direction and cycle diagnostics used as online, auditable reward sources without graph-formatted generation.
% 事后图探针证实推理并非线形，并可用于失败分析、结果预测、Best-of-$N$ 选择与推理期结构集成，但它们都作用在生成之后而非奖励内部。SARL 把步骤聚类成潜在功能，并在其"无向"转移图上奖励聚类性与短路径，刻意抽掉了方向性控制流，这一小世界先验与我们的语义支持关系互补。隐藏状态里的环可能是有用的循环访问，文本层面的自我强化重复则是解码病态。我们所说的环含义更窄：已提交推导中"论断最终支持自身"的循环论证。我们不惩罚探索、修订或重复措辞。因此新意不在于"把推理看成图"，而在于把原始方向与成环诊断当作在线、可审计的奖励源，且无需图格式生成。

\paragraph{Distillation and Reasoning Compression.}
\textit{\framework uses raw direction and cycle violations to elicit and filter teacher revisions, preserving dependencies that token budgets and post-projection pruning cannot identify, and is judged by the accuracy--length frontier plus independently audited topology preservation.} Knowledge distillation transfers teacher capability~\citep{hinton2015distilling}, and student-generated or preference-aligned variants reduce train--test mismatch~\citep{minillm2023,agarwal2024gkd,ko2024distillm,yang2026exopd,sessa2024bond,xu2025kdrl,ye2025gad}. Long-CoT compression prunes tokens, penalizes length, or controls budget~\citep{xia2025tokenskip,luo2025o1,arora2026training,aggarwal2025l1,muennighoff2025s1}; graph-based pruning removes weak branches from projected DAGs~\citep{yuan2026graphpruning}, and Reasoning Scaffolding distills semantic operations~\citep{wen2026scaffolding}. None of these targets a student's own missing dependency: a static teacher trace is identical for every student, and a length-only objective can shorten a trace by deleting support the conclusion needs.
% 知识蒸馏迁移教师能力，学生自采样或偏好对齐的变体缓解训练—测试分布错配；长 CoT 压缩则通过剪 token、惩罚长度或控制预算实现，基于图的剪枝从投影后的 DAG 上删除弱分支，Reasoning Scaffolding 蒸馏语义操作。这些方法都没有针对"该学生自己缺失的那条依赖"。\framework 用原始方向与成环违例来触发并筛选教师修订，从而保留 token 预算与投影后剪枝无法识别的依赖关系，并以准确率—长度前沿加独立审计的拓扑保持率来评估。
