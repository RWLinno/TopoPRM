\begin{abstract}
Long-form reasoning is generated as a token sequence even when a conclusion depends on several non-adjacent premises. Final-answer rewards cannot expose this support structure, whereas learned process verifiers add supervision and inference cost.
% 即便结论依赖多个非相邻前提，长文本推理仍会以令牌序列的形式生成。最终答案奖励无法揭示这种支撑结构，而经训练的过程验证器则会额外增加监督成本与推理开销。
We introduce \method, an implicit process reward that deterministically constructs a typed support DAG from ordinary reasoning text. The same graph supplies support-completeness and local-continuity signals to an outcome-anchored hierarchical reward, an asymmetric advantage estimator (ACE) that prevents structural credit from reversing correctness, and topology-guided on-policy distillation from a fixed same-scale teacher. The resulting policy emits unmodified text and requires neither graph decoding nor a verifier at inference.
% 我们提出\method，这是一种隐式过程奖励机制，可从普通推理文本中确定性地构建出类型化支撑DAG。该图同时为结果锚定分层奖励提供支撑完备性与局部连续性信号，为可避免结构性信用反转正确性的非对称优势估计器（ACE）提供信号，还用于基于固定同规模教师模型的拓扑引导在策略蒸馏。最终得到的策略输出未经修改的文本，且推理阶段既无需进行图解码，也不需要验证器。
On four mathematics benchmarks, the complete reward is the only reported variant that is non-dominated in both pass@1 and mean response length.
Relative to outcome-only GRPO, it improves pass@1 by $0.2$--$13.3$ points while reducing mean responses by 57--275 tokens. 
% 在四项数学基准测试中，完整奖励是唯一在 pass@1 和平均响应长度两项指标上均未处于劣势的公开变体。相较于仅针对结果的GRPO训练，该方法将平均响应长度缩短了 57 至 275 个 token，同时 pass@1 提升 0.2 至 13.3 个百分点。
Full exceeds the no-topology checkpoint by 3.0 points on OlympiadBench and 10.0 points on AIME~2024. %在 OlympiadBench 上，完整模型比无拓扑结构的检查点高出 3.0 分，在 AIME 2024 上高出 10.0 分。
\end{abstract}

%A frozen order-agnostic encoder recovers a directed support graph from an unchanged free-form trace, and projecting it onto a text-order directed acyclic graph (DAG) is where the signal comes from: the backward and cyclic edges the projection discards become two separate rewards, direction consistency and acyclicity, instead of one averaged topology score. A monotone 2-additive Choquet capacity aggregates them with outcome, format, and continuity. We show this reward equals its Shapley-matched additive control minus an explicit disagreement penalty, a contrast no reweighting reproduces. The same graph localizes teacher revisions for distillation, and the policy never emits graph tags.
% 我们提出 \method：冻结的顺序无关编码器从未改动的自由文本轨迹中恢复有向支持图，信号恰来自"投影"这一步——投影到文本序 DAG 时被丢弃的后向边与环边成为方向一致性和无环性两个独立奖励，而非单一的平均拓扑分数。单调 2-additive Choquet 容量将其与 outcome、format、continuity 聚合。我们证明该奖励等于其 Shapley 匹配加性对照减去一个显式分歧惩罚，这种区分是任何重新加权都无法复现的。同一张图还用于定位教师修订以引导蒸馏，策略本身从不输出图标记。
