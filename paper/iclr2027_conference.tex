\subsection*{Ethics statement}
The reported study uses public mathematics benchmarks and model-generated reasoning traces. It collects no personal or demographic data, includes no human-subject study, and uses no private benchmark to support a claim. The edge audit uses model annotators and is identified as such. The principal risk is methodological: a trace can exhibit coherent surface dependencies while containing an incorrect mathematical deduction. We therefore treat topology as a training-time process signal rather than a semantic verifier, report this failure mode directly, and retain final-answer verification as a separate reward source. The study is limited to mathematical reasoning; broader deployment claims would require domain-specific safety and validity evaluation.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Appendix~\ref{app:details} records the training, reward, and decoding configuration, and Appendix~\ref{app:dag} specifies graph construction and the audit protocol. The anonymized supplementary artifact contains source and configuration snapshots plus aggregate JSON files for the edge audit, controlled rerun, cross-backbone, structural, and shared-pool diagnostics. Table~\ref{tab:main_results} was transcribed from archived aggregate evaluation tables; its per-item outputs are not included, which we disclose as a reproducibility limitation. We distinguish single training runs, repeated decoding draws, and paired annotation statistics throughout, and we do not use incomplete experiments as evidence.
