\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

% 第一段：RLVR + PRM 共同把轨迹当线形 → 但推理实际是图 → 失配
Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has substantially advanced long chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models and now serves as a core mechanism for training mathematical and symbolic reasoning ability~\citep{wei2022chain,kojima2022large,deepseekr1,yeo2025demystify,shao2024deepseekmath,ke2026com}. From sparse final-answer rewards~\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k,deepseekr1} to process reward models (PRMs) that provide intermediate step-level supervision for improved reliability~\citep{lightman2023lets,wang2024math,uesato2022solving}, current approaches uniformly treat a reasoning trace as a \emph{linear sequence of steps}. In practice, long reasoning is rarely linear: in mathematical derivations, a later conclusion often depends on several non-adjacent premises, an intermediate equation is reused by multiple downstream derivations, and subgoals branch before merging into the final answer~\citep{besta2024graph}. Reconciling this structural mismatch has emerged as a core challenge for advancing reasoning supervision beyond linear credit assignment.
% RLVR 让 LLM 长 CoT 推理能力显著提升，并成为训练数学符号推理的核心机制。从稀疏的答案奖励到提供中间步骤监督的 PRM，现有方法都把推理轨迹当作线形步骤序列。但实际上长推理几乎从不是线形的：数学推导中后续结论常依赖若干非相邻前提、中间等式被多条后续推导复用、子目标在汇合到最终答案前先发生分支。这种"推理本质是图结构、监督却只看序列"的失配，是本文要解决的核心问题。

% 第二段：两类代表工作的具体不足，过去时
Recent reasoning models have made the macroscopic structure of inference clearer and more reliable, yet they remain confined to the CoT-as-result view. DeepSeek-R1~\citep{deepseekr1}, DAPO~\citep{yu2025dapo}, and Tulu~3~\citep{lambert2024tulu3} demonstrated that outcome-only rewards scale, but they could not distinguish a well-supported derivation from a trace that reached the same answer through unsupported intermediate claims, and they remained vulnerable to reward hacking~\citep{gao2023scaling,skalse2022defining}. On the PRM side, denser step-level signals have been obtained through Monte Carlo rollouts~\citep{wang2024math,luo2024omegaprm}, trained verifiers~\citep{zhang2025qwen25mathprm,thinkprm2025,genprm2026}, and implicit outcome-derived scoring~\citep{yuan2024implicitprm,she2025rprm}, yet all treat the reasoning trace as a \textbf{linear sequence of steps} and assign step-local credit, without modeling the \textbf{non-sequential dependency structure} in which a single intermediate result may support, or be supported by, several non-adjacent steps~\citep{wang2025com}. Token-level distillation~\citep{agarwal2024gkd} inherited the same linear bias by matching teacher distributions step by step.
% 近期推理工作让模型推理的宏观结构更清晰可靠，却仍停留在 CoT 结果层面。RLVR 一侧：DeepSeek-R1、DAPO、Tulu 3 证明仅凭结果奖励即可扩展，但无法区分"有支持的推导"与"经无依据中间断言到达同一答案的轨迹"，并且面临 reward hacking。PRM 一侧：Math-Shepherd、OmegaPRM、Qwen2.5-Math-PRM、ThinkPRM、GenPRM 提供更密集的步骤级信号，却普遍依赖人工标注、大量 rollout 标注流水线或须额外校准的独立验证器才能作为在线奖励。隐式 PRM 缓解了标注成本，但仍孤立打分每一步，把"第 t 步在 t−1 步之上"当作唯一的条件依赖。这些方法都未刻画跨非相邻步骤的隐式条件依赖；token 级蒸馏因逐步匹配教师分布而继承同样的线形偏置。

% 第三段：方法 + TGSD 合并。强调"用隐式拓扑依赖结构监督推理"
We propose \textsc{\method}, which supervises reasoning through its \emph{implicit topological dependency structure} rather than its surface order (Figure~\ref{fig:motivation}). The core idea is to extract, from any free-form reasoning trace, a DAG-shaped topology whose nodes are reasoning steps and whose edges encode surface-evidenced support relations such as expression reuse, variable propagation, claim reference, and explicit step citation. Two technical components turn this idea into a usable signal. First, a hybrid DAG scorer (rule-based patterns augmented with LLM-based inference) produces dual diagnostics that respectively measure \emph{global topology quality}, including orphan support, direction consistency, and acyclicity, and \emph{local continuity quality}, exposing the unsupported conclusions and redundant branches that final-answer rewards cannot observe. Second, we introduce \textsc{TGSD}, a post-training framework that shares the same topology diagnostics and hierarchically aggregates multi-source reward signals; at inference, it requires no human step labels, no separate verifier, and no graph-format generation. Through three stages of post-training, namely SFT warm-start, GRPO driven by a correctness-first hierarchical reward, and topology-guided on-policy self-distillation, \textsc{TGSD} yields a stronger and more token-efficient reasoning model.
% 我们提出 TopoPRM，用"隐式拓扑依赖结构"而非表面顺序来监督推理。核心思路：从任意自由形式推理轨迹中确定性地抽取 DAG 形式的拓扑结构，节点是推理步骤，边编码表层可证的支持关系（表达式复用、变量传递、断言引用、显式步骤引用）。两项技术让其落地：其一，确定性 DAG 打分器给出双重诊断——全局拓扑质量（孤立支撑、方向一致性、无环性等特性）与局部连贯质量——暴露结果奖励看不到的"无依据结论"与"冗余分支"。其二，提出 TGSD 框架共用同一套拓扑诊断与层次化聚合的多源奖励信息；推理时不需要人工标注、独立验证器或图格式生成。通过 SFT 热启动、正确性优先的层次化奖励驱动的 GRPO、以及拓扑引导的同策略自蒸馏三阶段后训练，得到效果更好且 token 更高效的推理模型。
Our contributions are threefold:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Reasoning topology as process supervision.} We formulate long CoT traces as implicit dependency DAGs and define topology-aware process signals that jointly score whether intermediate conclusions are \emph{supported}, \emph{directionally consistent}, and \emph{locally traceable}, capturing failure modes that outcome-only and step-isolated rewards both miss.
\item \textbf{A unified reward and post-training framework.} We instantiate the formulation as \textsc{\method}, an implicit PRM driven by topology DAG diagnostics, and embed it into \textsc{TGSD}, which couples SFT warm-start, hierarchical-reward GRPO, and topology-conditioned on-policy distillation through a single DAG Scorer.
\item \textbf{Empirical gains in accuracy and efficiency.} On multiple public math benchmarks at the 7B and 9B scales, \textsc{TGSD} consistently surpasses standard GRPO while compressing response length by up to 24\% on GSM8K and about 13\% on the four-primary-benchmark mean, and ablations confirm that topology-aware rewards offer supervision complementary to final-answer signals.
\end{enumerate}
% 三点贡献：(1) 把长 CoT 形式化为隐式依赖 DAG，定义"被支持 / 方向一致 / 局部可追溯"的拓扑感知过程信号，覆盖结果奖励与孤立步骤奖励都漏掉的失败模式；(2) 把该形式化落为 TopoPRM（无独立验证器的隐式 PRM）并嵌入 TGSD，三阶段共用单一抽取器；(3) 在 7B/9B 规模多个公开数学基准上稳定超越标准 GRPO，平均响应长度压缩 11.2%，消融证实拓扑奖励与结果奖励互补。
